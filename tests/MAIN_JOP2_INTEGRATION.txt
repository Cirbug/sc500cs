  Branch 11111 is not imported: its vsync/capture timestamp estimate does not

AF interpretation
  Scan remains 512-code coarse and 64-code fine, +/-512 around the best coarse
  point. This is reliability integration, not a new focus-search algorithm.
  A full scan takes approximately 8 seconds plus bus/scheduling delays; the gimbal
  intentionally holds position during this period. It is not a fixed completion
  deadline. Lens busy/recovery has a 1000 ms timeout and one recovery attempt.
  g_af_debug.reason: 7 complete, 8 I2C/init failure, 9 timeout, 10 manual override;
  1/2/3/4/5/6/13 identify queue/wait/recovery/scan/return/settle progress.
  Completion does not prove optical sharpness. Samples are time spaced, not tagged
  with camera frame IDs. Board behavior still requires physical validation.
